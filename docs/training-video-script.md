# Training videos: script

Two screen-recorded videos that teach Light Runner on the church's own stage, L1. The voice-over is in English; the screens are as they are, so the colours keep their Latvian names from the show.

| | Video 1: Running the lights at a service | Video 2: Setting up the lights and the show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| For | The live director and any volunteer | Whoever prepares the lights (the lighting lead) |
| Recorded on | The iPad (operator page) | A computer's browser (Patch, Settings, operator page; Timeline briefly) |
| Length | About 12 minutes | About 25 minutes, in chapters |
| After it, they can | Run a service, get the look they want for each moment, and get out of trouble | Patch the lights, draw the stage plan, make groups, colours, songs, quick looks and effects, and check it all before Sunday |

Video 2 builds the running order that Video 1 uses, so record Video 2 first (or build that running order before recording Video 1).

How to read the script: each scene has **Screen** (what is shown), **Do** (what the person recording taps or clicks), and **Say** (the voice-over). Times are rough. Text in a box like `this` is a caption or title card to add while editing.

## Before recording

- **Record at the church, with the lights on.** Lightkey's output off, then Light Runner's Settings › **Send DMX** on. In Test mode the top bar shows an amber "Not sending to the lights" pill on every shot, which is exactly what Video 1 teaches people to worry about.
- **The iPad:** brightness at about half, **Do Not Disturb** on, Auto-Lock off. Record with Control Centre › **Screen Recording** (hold it and switch the microphone off: the voice is recorded separately). Hold the iPad in landscape.
- **The computer:** a browser at full screen, zoom at 100 %, bookmarks bar hidden. On Windows, **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS records the screen; on a Mac, **Shift + Cmd + 5**.
- **Export the show first** (Settings › Show › **Export show file**). Video 2 changes the show; afterwards **Open a show file…** puts it back if you want.
- **The stage view** on the operator page: set it to **Audience** (the switch in its corner) and make it a little taller with its grip, so the lights read well on video. Switch to **Above** where the script says.
- **Optional:** a phone filming the real stage from the back of the room. A few seconds of the room changing colour, cut in after a tap, shows that the picture on the iPad is real.

## Video 1: Running the lights at a service

Recorded on the iPad, operator page. The running order holds **Basic lights**, **Walk-in**, **Worship**, **Sermon**, **Response** and **Prayer** (made in Video 2, chapter 7).

### Scene 1. Welcome (0:00, 40 s)

**Screen:** the operator page with **Basic lights** on stage. Cut to the room with the warm stage if you filmed it.
`Running the lights at a service`

**Say:** "This is Light Runner, the lighting controller for our stage. In the next twelve minutes you'll learn everything you need to run the lights at a service from this iPad: how to start each moment, how to change a look so it suits what's happening, and what to do if something looks wrong. You can't break anything. Every change fades gently, and one button always brings the stage back to a safe, warm light. Let's start."

### Scene 2. Before people arrive (0:40, 1 min)

**Screen:** the iPad home screen, then the operator page.

**Do:** tap the **Light Runner** icon on the home screen. Point to the top bar.

**Say:** "Open Light Runner from its icon on the home screen. It opens full screen. Look at the top bar first. On the left it says **On stage** and the name of what's on the lights now. Next to it there should be nothing else. If you see an amber pill, like 'Not sending to the lights' or 'Lights not answering', the lights aren't listening. Tap the pill: it says what's wrong and who to call. We'll come back to that at the end."

**Do:** tap **Basic lights** in the running order.

**Say:** "Now tap **Basic lights**. The stage comes up warm. Basic lights is the safe look. It's always first in the list, and you can always get back to it. Before people arrive, tap through each moment of the service once with the stage in view, so you know it all looks right."

### Scene 3. A tour of the page (1:40, 1 min 40 s)

**Screen:** the whole operator page. Highlight each part as it is named (a soft circle while editing).

**Say:**
- (stage view, top left) "Top left is a small picture of the stage, showing what the lights are doing right now. The switch in its corner shows it from **Above**, like a plan, or from the **Audience**, as the congregation sees it. It's only a picture. Touching it never changes the lights."
- (running order, left) "Under it is the **Running order**: the moments of the service, from top to bottom. The one on stage is lit up, and the one after it says **next**."
- (groups, middle) "In the middle are the **Groups**. Each group is a set of lights that work together. **Front lights** light the faces of whoever is speaking or singing. **Key lights** shine down from the truss. **Background** washes the back wall. **Drum cage** is the strip round the drums, and **B8 side bar** is the bar at the side. Each group has its colour, and a slider for how bright it is."
- (quick looks, top right) "Top right are **Quick looks**. They change only some groups, and leave the rest as they are."
- (bottom bar) "And along the bottom: **Master**, which dims everything at once, the **Flash** buttons, **Undo**, **Safe · Basic lights**, **Next**, and **Blackout**."

`Silti = warm · Vēsi = cool · Zils = blue · Violets = violet · Izslēgts = off` (or the full table from "The colours' names in the show")

**Say:** "The colours keep the names they had in our old lighting program, which are in Latvian. **Silti** is warm, **Vēsi** is cool, **Zils** is blue, **Violets** is violet, and **Izslēgts** means off."

### Scene 4. Running through a service (3:20, 1 min 40 s)

**Do:** tap **Walk-in**. Wait for the fade. Then tap **Next ▸** in the bottom bar.

**Say:** "To start a moment, tap it in the running order. Here's **Walk-in**: soft front light and a blue stage while people come in. Every change fades, so there's no need to rush. When it's time for the next moment, you don't have to find it in the list. The **Next** button at the bottom shows its name. Tap it."

**Do:** **Next ▸** to **Worship**. Show the stage view, and the room if filmed.

**Say:** "Now we're in **Worship**: warm front light for the band and violet on the back wall. **On stage** in the top bar says **Worship**, and **Next** now says **Sermon**."

**Do:** **Next ▸** to **Sermon**, then **Response**, then **Prayer**, pausing a moment on each.

**Say:** "**Sermon**: clear, neutral light on the speaker and a calm blue behind. **Response**: warm again, with orange behind. And **Prayer**: low and quiet. That's a whole service: tap the first moment, then **Next**, **Next**, **Next**."

### Scene 5. Getting the look you want (5:00, 2 min 30 s)

This is the heart of the video: how to change one thing about a look.

**Screen:** **Worship** on stage, the Groups panel.

**Say:** "Often the moment is right, but one thing needs to change. Maybe the preacher comes forward during a song, or the band wants it darker. You change a look one group at a time."

**Do:** drag the **Front lights** slider down to about 60 %.

**Say:** "To make a group dimmer, drag its slider. Here the front lights go down to sixty percent. Let go, and it stays."

**Do:** tap the **Background** colour button (it says **Violets**). The colour picker opens on **Colours**. Tap **Zils**, then **Tirkīzs**.

**Say:** "To change a group's colour, tap its colour button. You see the colours made for that group. Tap one, and the group fades to it. Try a few. Here's blue, and here's turquoise. Tap **Done** when you're happy."

**Do:** tap **Done**. Then tap the **Key lights** colour button and choose **Izslēgts**, then **Done**.

**Say:** "Off is a colour too. Here the key lights go off, so only the front lights and the back wall are on."

**Do:** point to **Save to Worship** at the top of Groups, and the **Back to saved** button that has appeared beside it.

**Say:** "You've now changed **Worship** on stage. That doesn't change the saved Worship. Next week it comes back as it was. If you want the old look back now, tap **Back to saved**. And if you want to keep your changes in Worship for good, hold **Save to Worship** for a second. Ask the lighting lead first if you're not sure."

**Do:** tap **Back to saved**.

**Say:** "Here are three changes worth remembering." Show each as a caption while saying it:
- `Speaker forward: Front lights up, Key lights on (Silti balts)` "Someone comes forward to speak: bring the front lights up, and the key lights on warm white."
- `Quieter moment: Background down to 30 %` "A quieter moment: pull the background down, not the front lights, so faces stay lit."
- `Darker room: Master down` "The whole room needs to be darker: use **Master** at the bottom. It dims every group together and keeps the look."

### Scene 6. Quick looks (7:30, 50 s)

**Do:** with **Sermon** on stage, tap the **Colours** tab in Quick looks, then a quick look (for example **All tirkīzs**, made in Video 2, chapter 8).

**Say:** "Quick looks are shortcuts. Each one changes just a few groups. This one turns the back wall, the drums and the side bar turquoise, and leaves the front lights alone, so the speaker stays lit. The tabs, **Plain**, **Colours** and **Effects**, are just where the buttons are kept. **Plain** has **All white** for when someone needs to see the whole stage."

### Scene 7. Effects and tempo (8:20, 1 min)

**Do:** tap the **Background** colour button, then the **Effects** tab, then **Wave**. Close with **Done**.

**Say:** "Some groups can also move. The background is four long bars, so it can run a wave, a chase or a colour fade along them. Tap the group's colour button, then **Effects**, and tap one. It runs straight away."

**Do:** tap **Tap** in the top bar four times in time with music (play a song quietly, or tap a steady beat).

**Say:** "Effects move with the music's tempo. Tap the **Tap** button in time with the band, four times or so, and the effect follows. The tempo shows next to it."

**Say:** "Effects never flash faster than three times a second, so they're safe for everyone in the room."

### Scene 8. Flash (9:20, 20 s)

**Do:** hold **Back** in the Flash row for two seconds, then let go.

**Say:** "Hold a **Flash** button and that group goes to full; let go and it comes back. **All** flashes everything. It's for a big moment at the end of a song."

### Scene 9. Prepare, then Go (9:40, 1 min)

**Do:** tap **Prepare** in the top bar (it turns violet). Tap **Response**, change the **Background** to **Rozā**. Show that the stage view changes but the room doesn't. Then tap **Go ▸**.

**Say:** "Sometimes you want to get the next look ready without the congregation seeing you try things. Tap **Prepare**. The top bar turns violet. Now every tap changes only a draft, which you see in the stage view, while the real lights stay exactly as they are. When the moment comes, tap **Go**, and the stage fades to your draft in one smooth change. Changed your mind? Tap **Live** instead, and the draft is thrown away."

### Scene 10. When something looks wrong (10:40, 1 min 10 s)

**Do:** change something by mistake (for example drag **Front lights** to 0), then tap **Undo**.

**Say:** "Everyone taps the wrong thing sometimes. **Undo** takes back the last change."

**Do:** tap **Safe · Basic lights**.

**Say:** "If you're lost, tap **Safe · Basic lights**. The stage comes back warm and still, every time. Then carry on from the running order."

**Do:** hold **Blackout** for one second. Then tap **Basic lights** to bring the lights back.

**Say:** "**Blackout** turns everything off. You have to hold it for a second, so it never happens by accident. A song, **Basic lights** or **Next** brings the lights back."

`Amber pill: tap it · Red banner: check the Wi-Fi · A light flickers: Basic lights, then tell the lighting lead`

**Say:** "If an amber pill appears in the top bar, tap it. It says what's wrong and who to call. A red banner saying 'Connection lost' means the iPad has lost the Wi-Fi. The lights keep their last look while it reconnects by itself. And if a light ever flickers or jumps, tap **Basic lights** and tell the lighting lead what you did just before."

### Scene 11. After the service, and goodbye (11:50, 30 s)

**Do:** tap **Basic lights** (or hold **Blackout**, as the room needs).

**Say:** "When the service is over, tap **Basic lights**, or **Blackout** if the room should go dark. Everything you did is saved by itself. You don't need to close anything. That's it. Thank you for serving with the lights."

`Tap a moment · Next · Change one group · Undo · Safe · Basic lights`

## Video 2: Setting up the lights and the show

Recorded in a browser on the computer: the Mac at the church, or a laptop on the church Wi-Fi at `http://192.168.0.195:8080`. Chapters are cut so each one can be watched on its own.

### Chapter 1. What we're building (0:00, 1 min 30 s)

**Screen:** a simple drawing: the Light Runner computer → the network → "NetWork Node 2" → the lights on stage; iPads beside the computer.
`The rig: Light Runner (the church Mac) → Art-Net node 192.168.0.114, universe 1 → the lights`

**Say:** "Light Runner runs on the church Mac. It sends to a small box called the Art-Net node, and the node drives the lights. The iPads and phones open Light Runner's page over the church Wi-Fi. In this video we'll set up the whole show for stage L1: bring in the lights, put them on a plan, group them, make colours, build the running order for a service, add quick looks and effects, and check it all before Sunday."

**Screen:** the operator page, then the page tabs in the top right.

**Say:** "Everything is done in four pages, and the tabs at the top right of every page switch between them: **Operator**, the volunteers' page; **Patch**, the lights and the stage plan; **Settings**; and **Timeline**. Patch and Settings ask for the programmer PIN when one is set."

### Chapter 2. Before you start (1:30, 1 min 30 s)

**Do:** open **Settings**. In **Show**, press **Export show file**. Show the file arriving in Downloads.

**Say:** "Before any big change, keep a copy. In Settings, under **Show**, press **Export show file** and keep the file somewhere safe. **Open a show file…** brings it back. Light Runner also keeps its last twenty saves by itself, under **Backups**, each with a **Restore** button."

**Do:** scroll to **DMX output**. Point to **Send DMX**.

**Say:** "**Send DMX** decides whether Light Runner is driving the lights. Only one program may send to the node at a time, so if Lightkey is running, switch its output off first, then switch **Send DMX** on. To give the lights back to Lightkey, switch **Send DMX** off first. While you build, you can leave it off: the stage view keeps showing what the lights would do."

**Do:** point to **Programmer PIN**.

**Say:** "Set a **Programmer PIN** here, four to six digits, so nobody changes the patch by accident. Keep it written down somewhere safe, not on the iPad."

### Chapter 3. Bringing in the lights from Lightkey (3:00, 2 min)

**Do:** Settings › Show › **Compare with a file…**. Choose the church's `.lightkeyproj`. Show the list of what is new or changed, then press **Apply selected**.

**Say:** "Our lights were already set up in Lightkey, so the fastest way is to bring them in from the Lightkey project. Press **Compare with a file…** and choose the project. Light Runner shows what it found: the fixture types, and each light with its name, its DMX address and its place on the stage. New and changed things are ticked. Press **Apply selected**. The current show is kept as a backup, and your PIN and output settings aren't touched. Groups, colours and songs don't come from Lightkey. We make those in a moment."

**Do:** open **Patch**. Scroll through the **Fixtures** table: P1–P4, B1–B6, L1, B8.

**Say:** "Here they are in Patch. **P1** to **P4** are the front panels. **B1** and **B2** are the bars on the truss, **B3** to **B6** the long bars at the back wall, **L1** the strip round the drums, and **B8** the side bar. They're all on universe 1, the one the node sends out."

### Chapter 4. A light by hand (5:00, 2 min 30 s)

Shows the route for a light that isn't in the Lightkey project. Use a spare light, or add one and delete it at the end of the chapter.

**Do:** in Patch, under **Open Fixture Library**, type `SonicPulse` and press **Search**. Tap **Stairville SonicPulse LED Bar 05**, then the mode with 23 channels (the one B8 uses).

**Say:** "If a new light arrives, find its type in the Open Fixture Library, which is a free database of lights. Type the model and press **Search**. Tap the light, then the **DMX mode** the light itself is set to. The number of channels must match what's set on the light."

**Do:** under **Add fixtures**, choose the type, type the name `Side bar 2`, **How many** 1, **Universe** 1, keep the suggested **Start address**. Press **Add**. Show the **DMX patch** map.

**Say:** "Then under **Add fixtures**, choose the type, give it a name, say how many, and the universe: at our church it's 1. Light Runner suggests the first free address, and the line under the form shows which channels it will use. Set the same address on the light itself. Press **Add**. In the map, each block is a light, and the same colour means the same type. If two lights overlap, they turn red."

**Do:** press **Save patch**, then **Identify** on the new light (Send DMX on).

**Say:** "Changes stay in the page until you press **Save patch** at the bottom. Then **Identify** flashes that one light white for three seconds, so you can find it in the room and see that its address is right."

**Do:** delete the practice light, then **Save patch**.

**Say:** "If a light isn't in the library, Light Runner lets you describe it channel by channel with **New fixture type**. The setup guide shows how."

### Chapter 5. The stage plan (7:30, 3 min)

**Screen:** the **Stage plan** at the bottom of Patch, with **From the audience** under it.

**Say:** "The stage plan is a picture of the stage from above, with the back of the stage at the top. The stage view on the iPads is drawn from it, so it's worth getting right. Under it, **From the audience** shows the same stage as the congregation sees it, and it changes as you edit."

**Do:** drag **B6** along the back wall to the left end, then **B5**, **B4**, **B3** to the right.

**Say:** "Drag each light to where it hangs. Left and right are as the congregation sees them. So B6, on the far left of the back wall, goes on the left here."

**Do:** click **B3**. Point to the shape (**Bar**) and drag its end handle to make it longer. Then Shift-click **B4**, **B5** and **B6**, and drag the end handle of one: all four change together. Press **Align › Middle**, then **Space evenly › Across**.

**Say:** "Click a light to change it. A bar has a handle at each end for its length. Select several by Shift-clicking or by dragging a box round them, and a handle on any of them changes them all the same way. **Align** and **Space evenly** line them up neatly."

**Do:** click **P1**. Point to **Hangs on** (**Stand**). Then click **L1**: shape **Outline**, **Hangs on** **Riser**. Then **B8**: an upright **Bar**, **Hangs on** **Side**.

**Say:** "**Hangs on** says where the light really is, for the view from the audience: the front panels stand on stands, the drum strip goes round a riser, and B8 is an upright bar at the side. When you leave it on **Guess**, Light Runner guesses from the plan, and you only need to set it when the guess is wrong. Watch the picture underneath follow."

**Do:** press **Save patch**. Then, just to show it, click **B1** and set its shape to **Beam**. Drag the round handle at the tip to aim it a little to the side. Show the beam leaning in **From the audience**.

**Say:** "A **Beam** is a light that throws a cone. Drag the round handle at its tip to aim it and set how far it reaches. From the audience, the beam leans the same way. Our key lights are bars, so I'll throw this away: **Discard** at the bottom takes the page back to the last save."

**Do:** press **Discard**.

### Chapter 6. Groups (10:30, 2 min)

**Screen:** Patch › **Groups**.

**Say:** "A group is what the volunteers control: one colour button and one slider for a set of lights. We'll make five."

**Do:** type `Front lights` next to **New group name**, press **＋ Add group**. In the group, **＋ Add fixture…** P4, P3, P2, P1 in that order. Then make **Key lights** (B1, B2), **Background** (B6, B5, B4, B3), **Drum cage** (L1) and **B8 side bar** (B8). Press **Save patch**.

**Say:** "Type a name and press **Add group**, then add its lights. The order matters: waves and chases run along the group in this order. So put the lights in the order they hang, from left to right as the congregation sees them. For the back wall that's B6, B5, B4, then B3. **‹** and **›** move a light, and **×** takes it out. A light can be in more than one group. Press **Save patch**."

`Front lights: P4 P3 P2 P1 · Key lights: B1 B2 · Background: B6 B5 B4 B3 · Drum cage: L1 · B8 side bar: B8`

### Chapter 7. Colours and the running order (12:30, 4 min)

**Screen:** the operator page in the browser.

**Say:** "The rest happens on the operator page, where the volunteers work. Each group has its own list of colours."

**Do:** tap the **Background** colour button. Show the list. Tap **＋ New colour**. Name `Deep violet`, pick a violet on the strip, **Saturation** high, **Brightness** 80 %, **Apply to** **Only Background**. Press **Save**.

**Say:** "Tap a group's colour button to see its colours. To make a new one, tap **New colour**, give it a name, and pick it on the strip. **Saturation** makes it paler or stronger, and **Brightness** sets how bright it is. The stage doesn't change while you're choosing. Press **Save**, and it's in the list."

`Silti = warm · Vēsi = cool · balts = white · Zils = blue · Violets = violet · Tirkīzs = turquoise · Oranžs = orange · Izslēgts = off`

**Do:** tap **Basic lights**. Set Front lights **Silti 3200K**, Key lights **Silti balts**, the other groups **Silti balts**. Hold **Save to Basic lights** for one second.

**Say:** "**Basic lights** is the safe look the volunteers can always go back to. Set it once: tap **Basic lights**, set each group to a plain warm light, and hold **Save to Basic lights**. Keep it simple and bright enough to see. It's how everyone gets out of trouble."

**Do:** build **Walk-in**: Front lights **Silti 50%**, Key lights **Izslēgts**, Background, Drum cage and B8 side bar **Zils**. Tap **＋ Add song**. Tap **Edit list**, tap the new "Song" name, type `Walk-in`, press Enter, then **Done**.

**Say:** "A moment in the running order is one saved picture of the whole stage. Get the stage looking right, then tap **Add song**. It's saved after whatever is on stage. Then **Edit list** lets you rename it. Tap the name, type, and press Enter."

**Do:** in the same way, add the others (speed up while editing, with a caption for each):
- `Worship: Silti 3200K · Silti balts · Violets · Violets · Violets`
- `Sermon: Neitrāli 5600K · Vēsi balts · Zils · Zils · Zils`
- `Response: Silti 3200K · Silti balts · Oranžs · Oranžs · Oranžs`
- `Prayer: Silti 50% · Izslēgts · Zils (Background at 40 %) · Zils · Zils`

**Say:** "Do the same for each moment of the service: Worship, Sermon, Response and Prayer. In **Edit list**, drag the handle to change the order; Basic lights always stays first."

**Do:** start **Worship**, change Background to **Deep violet**, hold **Save to Worship**.

**Say:** "To change a saved moment later, start it, change the stage, and hold **Save to** with its name."

**Do:** open the **Fade** menu in the top bar.

**Say:** "**Fade** in the top bar sets how long changes take. **Auto** uses each colour's own fade, and that's the right choice for most services."

### Chapter 8. Quick looks (16:30, 1 min 30 s)

**Do:** with **Sermon** on stage, set Background, Drum cage and B8 side bar to **Tirkīzs**. Tap **＋ Save stage as a look**. In **New quick look from the stage**, tap **Front lights** and **Key lights** so they say "left out". Show the grey name **All tirkīzs** in the **Name** box. Choose the **Colours** tab. Press **Save**.

**Say:** "A quick look changes only some groups. Set the stage, then tap **Save stage as a look**. Tap the groups it should leave alone: here the front and key lights, so the speaker stays lit whatever is on stage. Light Runner suggests a name from the colours, in grey. Leave the box empty to keep it, or type your own. Choose which tab it goes on, and press **Save**."

**Do:** press **Edit looks**, tap the new quick look, show the **Edit quick look** sheet, then **Done**.

**Say:** "**Edit looks** lets you rename a quick look, move it to another tab, or update it from the stage."

### Chapter 9. Effects (18:00, 3 min)

**Do:** tap the **Background** colour button, then **Effects**, then **＋ New effect**. Choose **Effect** **Wave**. Under **Colours**, add **Violets** and **Zils**. Show the grey name **Violets and zils wave**. Set **Speed** to 4 beats, **Direction** **Left → right**. Press **Save**. Tap it in the list to run it.

**Say:** "Effects are moving looks for a line of lights, like our four bars on the back wall. Tap the group's colour button, then **Effects**, and **New effect**. Pick the kind: a wave here. Choose its colours, in the order they play. The strip at the top shows it moving, but the stage doesn't change until you save. The name is suggested from the colours and the effect. **Speed** is in beats, so it follows the music's tempo: four beats is one round every bar. Press **Save**, then tap it to run it."

**Do:** tap **Drum cage**'s colour button, then **Effects**. Show "Effects need a line of lights".

**Say:** "A group of one light, like the drum strip, can't run a wave. It says which groups can."

**Do:** in the top bar, tap **Tempo** and type `72`, then press **Tap** a few times instead.

**Say:** "Set the tempo by typing it, or tap **Tap** in time with the band. A song keeps the tempo it was saved with."

**Do:** start **Worship**, run the wave on **Background**, and hold **Save to Worship**.

**Say:** "To keep an effect in a moment, run it on the group and save the moment as before."

**Say:** "Light Runner keeps effects safe. Nothing flashes more than three times a second. If an effect would go faster, it's slowed down by itself."

### Chapter 10. Prepare, controllers and timelines, in one minute (21:00, 1 min)

**Screen:** quick cuts: **Prepare** on the operator page, an APC mini, the Timeline page with the Christmas intro.

**Say:** "Three more things, each covered in the guides. **Prepare** lets anyone build the next look without touching the lights, then **Go**. And the **Timeline** page lays lights along a recording, like the Christmas intro, so they change by themselves while it plays."

### Chapter 11. Check before Sunday (22:00, 2 min)

**Do:** Lightkey output off; Settings › **Send DMX** on. On the operator page, tap **Prepare** and tap through every moment, watching the stage view. Then **Live** and tap through again with the room in view. Hold **Flash** for each group.
