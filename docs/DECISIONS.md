# Decisions

Choices made while building Light Runner, newest first, with the reason. Add an entry whenever the build departs from [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) or a choice would surprise someone reading the code.

| Date | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-20 | **Blind mode: the calls behind [the design](superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-blind-mode-design.md).** (1) **Undo follows what the page is working on:** in Blind it takes back the draft's last change; once Blind is off (or after Go) it works on the stage as usual, and one Undo takes a Go back. (2) **Smoke and Next stay live:** the smoke slider and Puff act on the real stage and Go never changes smoke; Next always starts the next song on the real stage, worked out from the real stage, as do Master, Flash, Blackout and Fade (Go uses the live Fade). (3) **Per tablet:** each tablet's draft is its own, kept by the server and sent only to that tablet; it ends when the tablet reloads or loses its connection, and there is no Blind on the phone. (4) The top bar keeps showing the real stage (its name, "changed", fading, Flash, Blackout), with a faint violet strip and Go while Blind is on; the running order, Details and the stage view show the draft, and the song on the real stage keeps a quiet outline. (5) A drag that starts in Blind stays in Blind: if Blind goes off under the finger, the rest of the drag is dropped rather than sent to the stage. | Blind is for getting the next moment ready during a service; the controls that matter for the room right now (dimming, accents, going dark, smoke, the next song) must never be hidden in a draft. Undo acting on what the page shows is what a volunteer expects. The real lights keep doing what they do, so the bar that describes them stays true; violet is the one tint not already meaning something (blue is selected or Flash, amber a problem, red Blackout). |
| 2026-10-20 | **Colour editing: the shared colours (the palette) are edited through a "Change it everywhere" switch in the colour editor, with no separate page, and brightness belongs to each colour, not to the palette.** With the switch on, a new colour changes the shared colour for every colour that points to it; with it off, only this colour changes and it no longer points to the shared one. A brightness change always stays on the colour being edited (it is the colour's layer intensity), so the palette holds colours, not levels. Fade is the colour's own (Instant, 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 s, plus any other value it already has); the operator's Fade in the top bar still overrides it. | Volunteers never meet the word "palette": they see a blue that is the same in every group and want to change all of them or just one. A palette entry has no level, so a shared brightness would mean something different in each group. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Effects: the calls behind [the design](superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-effects-design.md).** (1) A group's lights are one line of segments (each repeated set of colour channels in a fixture type is one segment), so a wave runs across the four background bars as one image; Patch has a Reversed switch per fixture. (2) One effect per group at a time; layering stays Phase 2b. (3) The engine keeps any segment from flashing (rising and falling by more than 20 %) more than 3 times a second by running that effect at half speed, again if needed (it allows 95 % of the exact limit, so frames a few milliseconds off can't squeeze a 4th flash into a second); the check sits in the effect, before crossfades, Master and Flash. (4) Effect speed counts in beats of the song's tempo (or Free, in seconds); a song without a tempo runs at 120. (5) The old speed dial is gone from the page; the `speed` command and `POST /api/speed` stay as an overall multiplier on the tempo. (6) Ready-made effects are added to each light group the first time this version runs, named in English, and are editable. | The owner wants Lightkey-like movement without Lightkey's channel cap, with one effect fitting slow and fast songs. Photosensitivity is a safety limit, so it can't depend on the settings a volunteer picks. |
| 2026-10-19 | **One stage clock for effects.** Every group's effect starts from the stage's beat clock (for Free effects, the stage's seconds), then moves by its own speed; a live tweak (a `continues` send) keeps the running position. | Two effects started at different moments must still line up with the beat, and a volunteer's tweak must not restart the pattern. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Undo puts back the tempo the stage had before the undone step.** So undoing a song start brings back the earlier song's tempo, and undoing a tempo change takes that change back. Tempo changes made within 3 s of each other (tapping along, a held − or +) are one Undo step, so one Undo takes back the whole run: on the stage, consecutive `tempo` commands (any other step in between, or an Undo, ends the run); in Preview, consecutive `itemTempo` edits of the same song. (The design said Undo would leave the tempo alone except after the tempo control; this replaces that.) | Undo puts the whole stage back as it was, and a song's tempo belongs to the song. Keeping the tempo apart would leave the old song's effects at the new song's speed. |
| 2026-10-19 | **The tempo control sits in the top bar next to Fade, and on the phone at the top of Groups.** It shows the tempo (a menu with ×2, ÷2, − and + that holds to repeat, 1 bpm a step, 40 to 240) beside a 44 px Tap button (the average of the last 4 gaps; a gap over 2 s starts over; it sets the tempo from the second tap). Live it sends `tempo` and changes the stage; in Preview it sends `itemTempo` and changes the selected song's saved tempo. Starting a song sets the tempo (its own, or 120); quick looks, Basic lights and Blackout leave it. A held − or + sends at most 4 a second. | A quiet readout fits the top bar's other items, and a volunteer taps along with the band. Tempo changes made within 3 s of each other are one Undo step (see the Undo row), and a hold still shouldn't send one a frame. |
| 2026-10-19 | **The effect settings row shows only the settings the chosen effect uses** (no Waves on Breathe, no Size on a wave with several colours), and names Size for what it does (Tail, Amount, Depth). | Quiet controls: a volunteer shouldn't see knobs that do nothing. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Ready-made effects need two coloured swatches.** A multi-segment group gets them only when its colour list has at least two non-white colours; white-only groups get none (the owner can add one). | The design says each effect uses the first two or three coloured swatches; an effect of two whites would look like nothing. |
| 2026-10-19 | **In Preview, a change on the operator page is saved straight into the selected item**, one Undo step per tap or per drag (when the finger lifts), with nothing sent to the lights. There is no Save or Back to in Preview. Live, a change goes on stage and reaches the item only on Save. Groups the item doesn't set say "not set" and their dimmer can't move until they get a colour; Basic lights' dimmers stay at full. Save stage as quick look, the colour list's Edit and Add to colours are hidden, since they work on other things than the item. Preview covers running-order items only and saves straight into the item, so it is narrower than the spec's Blind mode (presets, manual changes and a Go step), which stays a later idea. In Preview, a group Basic lights doesn't list shows "Basic light" at 100 % with its dimmer locked, because starting Basic lights really puts those groups on the built-in light. Preview turns off after a reload or when the tablet loses its connection. | Preview has nothing on stage to save from, and the point of it is to fix a later song while an earlier one runs. Saving on release keeps a drag from making dozens of steps. |
| 2026-10-19 | **"Compare with a file" never changes whether a group is a smoke machine**: a changed group keeps this show's kind, and kind alone is not listed as a difference. A new group takes the file's kind. | Show files made from Lightkey know nothing of smoke, so every group in them reads as a light. Taking one would quietly hand the hazer back to Basic lights. |
| 2026-10-19 | **A group can be a smoke or haze machine** (`kind: 'smoke'`, set in Patch; old shows read every group as a light). | The owner asked about a smoke machine. Patched as a plain dimmer, Basic lights would turn it on at full, Flash All would fire it, and looks would switch it on and off. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Only four things change the smoke level: its own slider, Puff, Basic lights (off) and Blackout (off).** The slider is the `groupLevel` command, so the HTTP API's `POST /api/group/<smoke group>/level/<percent>` sets it too (Companion, for example). Looks, quick looks, Back to look, Undo and Master never do. A smoke group never counts as "changed". | Starting a song must never fill the room, or clear it, by surprise. The volunteer decides when there is smoke. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Saves leave smoke groups out** (Save to an item, Save to Basic lights, Add song, Save stage as quick look; the quick-look sheet doesn't offer them), and the engine ignores a smoke group listed in an older or imported look. | Same rule from the other side: a look can't hold smoke, so it can't start it. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Basic lights, Back to look on Basic lights and Blackout turn smoke off over 1 s**, never to the safe light. | Basic lights is the recovery button and must give plain light, not haze. 1 s keeps the 5 % rule (3.75 % per frame at worst). |
| 2026-10-19 | **Flash All never includes smoke.** The page leaves smoke groups out of All and gives them no Flash button, and the engine ignores a smoke group in any flash that names several groups. | A Flash is a light accent. An HTTP or Companion "flash everything" must not puff either. |
| 2026-10-19 | **A smoke group's own flash is Puff:** full while held, ignoring Master and its slider (which keeps its level); let go and it goes back. No Puff during Blackout. It uses the Flash mechanism, so it also ends when the tablet disconnects or misses the 2 s check. | A short burst on demand is how a smoke machine is used live. A lost tablet must never leave it running at full. |
| 2026-10-19 | **A Puff ends by itself after 15 s**, even with the button still down; let go and hold again for another. | A finger resting on the button, or a tablet that keeps answering while forgotten on a music stand, must not fill the room. |
| 2026-10-19 | **The smoke slider is refused during Blackout** ("Blackout is on."); the page leaves the slider where it was. Light dimmers still move. | Blackout means nothing on stage, smoke included, until the lights come back, the same as Puff. |
| 2026-10-19 | **A group that stops being a smoke group comes back with its dimmer at full** and no preset (dark until a look, Basic lights or a colour sets it). The Patch switch says that Basic lights and looks will then drive the machine. | Its old smoke level means nothing as a light dimmer. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Master doesn't dim smoke.** | Master dims the lights. Turning the lights down for prayer shouldn't change the haze. |
| 2026-10-19 | **After a restart smoke is always off.** A group that becomes a smoke group in Patch also starts off. | Smoke must never start by itself: after a crash, a power cut or a programmer's change, nobody may be watching. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Smoke output is linear (no gamma)**, on the fixture's dimmer channel, or its only channel for 1-channel types; other channels keep their fixed values. A fixture in a smoke group is driven only by smoke groups, so a light group it is also in can't light it. Colours, presets and manual are refused for smoke. | A smoke machine's level is an output amount, not a brightness. A hazer added to a light group by mistake must stay safe. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Open Fixture Library fog and haze output channels become the dimmer**; a channel that mixes fog with other functions is parked at 0, whatever its default. Patch warns on a smoke group whose fixture type has no dimmer channel (several channels), and on a light group holding a fixture named or typed like a smoke machine. | OFL calls a smoke machine's output "Fog"; it used to become a fixed "other" channel, so a machine with a fan channel was never driven, and a non-zero default would have run it all the time. Once it is a dimmer, an unmarked group would drive it, hence the warning. |
| 2026-10-19 | **The top bar says "Smoke: N %" quietly while any smoke machine runs** (Puff shows 100 %), and keeps saying it during a DMX problem. | One glance shows the haze is on, without a warning colour. The DMX box holds its last frame when it stops answering, so the smoke may still be running. |
| 2026-10-19 | **An Any colour drag (the strip or a White, Amber or UV slider) is one Undo step and follows the finger.** The first send of a gesture (a tap, or the start of a drag) fades like a swatch (the operator's fade time, or the default) and adds the Undo step; the sends after it carry `continues`, fade in 0.2 s each from what is on stage, like Master and manual, and add no Undo step while the group still runs the drag's live colour. Any Any colour send without `continues` starts a new drag, so two drags never share a step. The jump check treats continued sends like manual moves. | A 2 s drag made about 40 Undo steps, pushing a Save out of the list, and each send restarted a 3 s fade, so the stage only caught up after the finger stopped. |
| 2026-10-19 | **White, amber and UV are part of every light**, next to red, green and blue, and mix and fade like them. Presets, palette colours and Any colour (`groupColour`) can set them. A fixture without an amber channel makes amber (about #FFBF00) from red and green; UV is only sent to UV channels: for a fixture without one the engine takes UV out of each preset's light before mixing (intensity lowered to what is left, UV alone is dark), so its intensity is what it shows and the stage view draws no violet there. The jump check judges how bright each fixture looks (its brightest written colour channel × intensity), not only intensity. Amber makes colour-temperature fixtures warmer. White is still not taken out of RGB automatically. | The SixBar 500's amber and UV LEDs were never lit, and Any colour could never use white. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Live colour ids carry white, amber and UV as whole percentages**, zeros left out: `live:<group>:<#hex>:w40:a20:u5`. Ids without them are unchanged. Going back to an older build loses the extras: it can't restore such ids and drops amber and UV from saved presets. | Old saved stage state and tests keep working; 1 % is the sliders' step. |
| 2026-10-19 | **The White, Amber and UV sliders sit side by side under Any colour**, only for groups with those channels, and show a running preset's levels scaled by its intensity. The Any colour strip sends its colour with the sliders' levels only when the group already runs an Any colour pick or a slider was moved since the picker opened; otherwise the pick is the plain colour. | Three stacked sliders pushed UV out of sight on the iPad; scaling keeps a slider move from changing the brightness; from Basic lights (White 40 %) a tap on red should still give red, not pink. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Output addresses are IP addresses.** Art-Net routes, sACN routes with a receiver, and the default address when it is saved from Settings must be IPv4 (older shows holding a name still load). | The node watch recognises replies by address, so a name always looked "not answering"; and a name is looked up on every packet. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Saving Patch keeps the live settings.** Only the patch, groups and the rest of the show come from the Patch page. | A Patch page left open holds an old copy of the settings; saving it could put back old routes or turn Send DMX on again after handing the lights to another desk. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Output routing: one default output plus per-universe overrides.** `settings.output` stays the output for every universe; the optional `settings.routes` gives single universes their own output. Universes routed to the same box share one output. Routes for universes nothing is patched on open nothing. `LIGHT_RUNNER_OUTPUT=none` turns routes off too. | Existing shows keep working unchanged, and the common case (one node) needs no extra settings. |
| 2026-10-19 | **A route never renumbers a universe.** The Patch universe is the Art-Net port-address in ArtDmx (as Find nodes lists it) and the sACN universe number (0 goes out as 1). | That was already the rule for the single output; one number everywhere is easiest to check against the node. |
| 2026-10-19 | **A USB interface (ENTTEC) gets exactly one universe.** Two universes routed to the same USB port, or a route to the port the default output uses, are refused with a plain message. When several universes fall to a default USB output, it sends the lowest (as before) and Settings says which are not sent. | A DMX USB Pro has one DMX output. |
| 2026-10-19 | **The node watch polls every unicast Art-Net node in use** (default and routes) from one socket, three misses per node. Each problem names its node; several are joined into one message for the amber pill. With several outputs, the worker also names an output it can't send to. | Two boxes can fail separately; the volunteer needs to know which. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Basic lights resets dimmers to full, so Basic lights doesn't keep dimmer levels.** Tapping it fades every group dimmer to full over 2 s (with Master and speed), not to saved levels. Save to Basic lights stores every group at full, and Basic lights shows as changed when any group's dimmer isn't at full. Back to look on Basic lights puts back its presets with every dimmer at full, and leaves Master alone. | The spec says Basic lights recovers the stage, as Safe did. A dimmer left low by Basic lights would not be a recovery. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Basic lights is always first and always exists, set by Save, not in Settings.** The Settings "Safe button fades to" choice is gone. Every show that is loaded or saved is checked: a missing Basic lights comes back as an empty look, first in the running order and nowhere else in it. | The operator page relies on it. Compare with a file or the old Settings choice could take it away or point it at a song. |
| 2026-10-19 | **The server checks each tablet every 2 s and drops a silent one, ending its flash.** A tablet that misses two checks in a row is dropped, as if it had closed the page. | A tablet that falls off the Wi-Fi never says goodbye, so its Flash would stay on. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Undo of a Save keeps the hidden colour presets** ("Custom", "Basic light", "Off") that the Save made. | The stage may still be running one. Without it the next Save is refused and a restart leaves the group dark. Hidden presets do no harm. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Basic lights replaces Safe.** It is the Safe look, always first in the running order. Tapping it resets Master and every dimmer to full, as Safe did; groups the safe look doesn't list use the built-in warm light. | Volunteers need one obvious "plain lights" button, and it already existed. It should behave as it always did. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Looks remember group levels.** The level fades with the look's fade (`LEVEL_FADE_FACTOR` 1). At a 1 s fade the worst step is 4.95% per frame, just under the 5% rule. | A song should come back as it was saved, dimmers included. A late frame could push a step over 5%; if that shows up, raise the factor to 1.5. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Save is a hold (1 s) with no PIN.** It is refused while Blackout is on ("Blackout is on. Bring the lights back, then save."). | The volunteer lead is the one who saves and Undo covers a slip; a PIN would slow the service down. Saving in Blackout would store a dark look. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Unsaved changes are dropped when another item is started.** | Changes belong to the look on stage. Carrying them over would surprise the next song. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Saving turns an Any colour into a hidden custom preset, a group on the built-in warm light into a hidden "Basic light" preset, and a group left dark by Blackout into a hidden "Off" preset** (one per group, reused). After Save the engine swaps to the saved preset with no visible change, so "changed" clears. | Looks store presets, not raw colours. Hidden presets keep the palette the volunteers see short. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Back to look on Basic lights restores the saved presets with every dimmer at full, but leaves Master alone.** | Master isn't saved with a look; it is the live "everything dimmer" control. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Quick looks set only their groups.** Today's imported looks became the first quick looks. | A quick look like "Blue" should change colours without touching what the other groups are doing. Nothing the volunteers had is lost. |
| 2026-10-19 | **New songs are numbered after the highest "Song N".** | Deleting a song must not make the next one reuse a number still in the list. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Operator edits keep the programmer's backups:** only the first operator edit after a programmer save or a restart makes a backup. | Otherwise a busy service would push every programmer backup out of the 20 kept. |
| 2026-10-19 | **A programmer change (Settings, import, restore) clears show edits from Undo.** | Undo must not bring back an older show or an older PIN. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Next is disabled while editing the running order;** Blackout still works there. After Blackout, Next carries on from the item before it. | A stray tap on Next while rearranging should not change the lights. Blackout must always work. Carrying on from the item before keeps the service in order. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Flash is white and ignores Master and the dimmers, but not Blackout.** It snaps in and out (outside the jump check). A tablet that disconnects while holding Flash has its flash put out, sparing groups another tablet holds. | Flash is an accent, as in Lightkey, and has to be seen. A lost tablet must never leave lights stuck on. Short snaps are excluded from the 5% rule like fades under 1 s. |
| 2026-10-19 | **A Flash release from one tablet also puts out a group another tablet is holding** (only a disconnect spares other tablets' groups). | Two people flashing the same group at once is rare; keeping Flash simple matters more. |
| 2026-10-19 | **Take over (Send DMX back on) stays in Settings, behind the PIN.** | Handing the lights between Lightkey and Light Runner is a programmer's job, not something to hit by accident during a service. |
| 2026-10-18 | **Keep Lightkey project import** (command-line importer and Settings > Compare with a file). | Requested by Ilmārs: Lightkey is where the rig is set up today, and its projects are how changes arrive. |
| 2026-10-18 | The whole Open Fixture Library is downloaded from its GitHub archive (about 3 MB) into `data/ofl/database.json`; updates compare commit ids and list changes before installing. | The church may have no internet; OFL is small enough to keep whole; updates should never change a running show silently. |
| 2026-10-18 | Fixture types keep their channels when the database updates; to use a corrected definition, re-add it from Patch. | Changing channel layouts under a live show could break looks. |
| 2026-10-18 | Profiles allow up to 512 channels (spec implied fewer). | Some OFL pixel modes use up to 256 channels. |
| 2026-10-18 | "Compare with a file" ticks new and changed items by default, never removals; PIN and output settings are never imported. | Nothing should disappear or lock someone out by accident. |
| 2026-10-18 | Lightkey projects are compared on the patch only. | Lightkey has no groups, presets or looks in Light Runner's sense. |
| 2026-10-18 | Fade time is one shared setting on the server (Auto = each look's own), shown as a quiet "Fade" menu. | All tablets and Companion must agree; the user found a row of buttons too loud. |
| 2026-10-18 | Master fader always visible; group dimmers in a panel behind a fader-width "Groups" button that shows mini level bars and "N dimmed". | Keeps the bottom row small however many groups there are, without hiding a dimmed group. |
| 2026-10-18 | Undo keeps the last 20 look/preset changes on the server and fades back over 2 s. | Most common recovery after a wrong tap; shared by every device. |
| 2026-10-18 | Groups are named by the user: Front lights P1–P4, Key lights B1–B2, Background B3–B6, Drum cage L1, B8 side bar; P5 left out. | Told by Ilmārs. |
| 2026-10-18 | sACN output added (spec listed it as a non-goal). | Requested by Ilmārs. |
| 2026-10-18 | Programmer pages are plain HTML/JS, not React; the server uses `node:http` + `ws`, not Fastify. | Fewer dependencies, no build step on the church Mac. |
| 2026-10-18 | The node watch polls the Art-Net node (ArtPoll) and reports "not answering" after 3 misses, or "another desk is also sending" when the node says it is merging. | UDP sends can't fail visibly; a wrong IP once sent DMX nowhere unnoticed. |
| 2026-10-18 | Safe and Blackout don't hand shared fixtures between groups, and Safe resets master, speed and levels over 2 s (the wash still fades in 1 s). | Two simultaneous curves multiplied made jumps just over 5% per frame. |
| 2026-10-18 | The 5% rule is measured on intensity, and fades shorter than 1 s are excluded from the jump check. | A full 0–100% fade under 0.75 s can't stay below 5% per frame at 40 fps. |
| 2026-10-18 | Fade entries leave the mix only when their weight reaches 0 (cap of 8), not at 1% as the spec says. | Dropping at 1% made a visible ~1% dip at the end of every fade. |
| 2026-10-18 | Colour mixes as emitted light (weighted by intensity), then is scaled so its brightest channel is 1. | Fading up from dark keeps the target colour instead of passing through white; red to blue stays bright. |
| 2026-10-18 | CCT-only fixtures (AS600B) get a colour temperature estimated from the colour's blue/red ratio; the CCT channel is linear (no gamma). | Lets the same warm/cool presets drive RGB and white-only fixtures. |
| 2026-10-18 | A fixture in two groups follows the most recent change to either group, handing over with the same fade. | Spec rule ("most recent change wins") without a snap. |
| 2026-10-18 | Restart restores each group's preset (not just the look) with no fade. | Groups can differ from the look; the node holds the last frame, so a fade would dip. |
| 2026-10-18 | Atomic writes and backups from the start (spec put backups in Phase 4). | A corrupt show file would stop a service. |
| 2026-10-17 | Engine is a pure package; DMX runs on a worker thread with a drift-free clock. | Same code for output, tests and tools; a slow request can't delay a frame. |
